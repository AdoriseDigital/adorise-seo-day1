Stack Recommendations by Business Stage

Solo Founder / Micro Business (<5 people, <$1M ARR)

  • Core LLM: ChatGPT Plus ($20) + Perplexity Pro ($20) = $40/mo
  • Automation: n8n self-hosted (free) or Make Free
  • Content: Notion AI ($10) or ChatGPT for writing
  • Meetings: Fathom Free
  • Total: ~$50-70/mo

Growing Team (5-25 people, $1-10M ARR)

  • Core LLM: ChatGPT Team ($25/user) or Claude Team ($30/user)
  • Automation: n8n Cloud ($50-200) or Make Pro ($29+)
  • Content: Jasper ($49) or Copy.ai ($49) for marketing
  • Meetings: Fireflies Business ($18/user)
  • Coding: GitHub Copilot Business ($19/dev)
  • Total: ~$150-400/mo base + per-seat

Scaling Company (25-200 people, $10M+ ARR)

  • Core LLM: Enterprise agreements (Claude, OpenAI, or multi-model)
  • Automation: n8n Enterprise / Workato / Tray.io
  • Data/Analytics: Custom RAG, internal AI platform
  • Governance: Audit logs, SSO, data residency, admin controls
  • Total: $2,000-20,000+/mo

Decision Framework: How to Choose

  1. Define 3-5 primary use cases (not "AI" — be specific: "draft cold emails," "summarize sales calls")
  2. Run 2-week trials on top 2 tools per use case with real work
  3. Measure time saved + output quality vs. current process
  4. Check integration requirements (does it connect to your CRM, CMS, etc.?)
  5. Evaluate data privacy — where does your data go? Training opt-out?
  6. Calculate total cost including implementation, training, ongoing
  7. Start with one category, prove value, then expand

FAQ

Should I pay for multiple LLM subscriptions?
For most teams: one primary (ChatGPT Team or Claude Team) + Perplexity Pro for research. Specialized tools (Cursor, Jasper, Fireflies) serve different workflows. Avoid redundant chat subscriptions.
Is self-hosted n8n really free?
Software: yes. Infrastructure: $5-50/mo for VPS. Your time: 5-20 hrs setup. At 10K+ executions/mo, self-hosted saves $100-500/mo vs. cloud. Worth it if you have technical capacity.
What about data privacy with these tools?
ChatGPT Team/Claude Team/Enterprise: data not used for training, SOC2, DPA available. Free tiers: data may be used for training. Self-hosted (n8n, local LLMs): full control. Always check current policies before putting sensitive data in.
How do I avoid tool sprawl?
Audit quarterly. Kill tools with <2 hrs/week usage. Consolidate to platforms (Notion AI replaces separate writing tool). Enforce "one tool per category" policy. Centralize procurement.

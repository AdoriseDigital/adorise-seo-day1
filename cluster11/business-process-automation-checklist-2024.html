FAQ

How long does the full audit take?
2-4 hours for a 20-person company. Interview 3-5 process owners. Don't guess—ask "walk me through this step by step."
What if a process scores high on complexity?
Break it into sub-processes. Automate the 80% happy path first. Keep human-in-the-loop for exceptions. Complexity often comes from trying to automate everything at once.
Should I automate processes that will change soon?
If changing in <3 months, wait. If changing in 6+ months, automate now—the ROI during those 6 months pays for the rebuild. Use modular workflow design to minimize rework.
How do I get buy-in from process owners who fear automation?
Frame as "eliminate the boring parts so you can do the valuable parts." Show them the time savings calculation. Involve them in design. Guarantee no job loss—redeployment to higher-value work.

Strategy 1: AI SDR for Top-of-Funnel Outbound

What It Automates

List building → Research → Email writing → Sequence management → Reply handling → Meeting booking

Workflow

  1. ICP Definition: 20+ firmographic/technographic criteria in Apollo/Clay
  2. List Building: Auto-refresh weekly, 500-2,000 prospects/month
  3. Research Enrichment: AI scans 10-K, news, LinkedIn, tech stack, hiring
  4. Email Generation: GPT-4 writes personalized first touch + 4 follow-ups per prospect
  5. Deliverability: Domain warm-up, rotation, bounce handling, spam monitoring
  6. Reply Management: AI classifies replies (interested/meeting/not interested/oob) → routes
  7. Booking: Calendly links embedded, AI handles scheduling ping-pong

  • Open rate: 45-65% (vs 15-25% generic)
  • Reply rate: 8-15% (vs 1-3% generic)
  • Meeting booked rate: 2-4% of emails sent
  • Cost per meeting: $150-400 (vs $800-2,000 human SDR)

Strategy 3: Automated Meeting Prep & Intelligence

What It Automates

Calendar scan → Company/People research → Competitive intel → Talking points → Delivery

Prep Packet Contents (Auto-Generated)

  • Company: Revenue, funding, headcount, tech stack, recent news
  • Attendees: LinkedIn, role, tenure, mutual connections, recent posts
  • Account history: Past deals, tickets, usage, health score
  • Competitive: Known competitors in account, win/loss vs them
  • Talking points: 3-5 personalized hooks based on research
  • Risks: Budget cycles, champion changes, contract renewals

Delivery

Slack DM / Email / Notion page — 1 hour before meeting. AE walks in prepared, every time.

Strategy 4: Proposal & Contract Automation

What It Automates

Deal stage change → Pricing engine → Template merge → Doc generation → E-sign → CRM update

Components

  • Pricing Engine: Rules-based (volume, term, features) → calculated price
  • Template Library: Modular sections (scope, timeline, terms, security)
  • Data Merge: CRM fields + pricing + selected modules → PandaDoc/DocuSign
  • Approval Workflow: Discount >20% → manager approval via Slack
  • E-Sign + Sync: Signed → PDF to CRM → deal stage update → invoice trigger

Time Savings

4-6 hours → 15 minutes per proposal. 85% reduction. Zero pricing errors.

Strategy 6: Conversation Intelligence & Coaching

What It Automates

Auto-Extracted Insights

FAQ

Will AI SDR replace my human SDRs?
No. AI SDR handles volume, speed, consistency for SMB/mid-market outbound. Human SDRs handle: enterprise accounts, complex qualification, relationship building, strategic accounts. Best model: AI SDR feeds human AEs.
What's the minimum deal size for AI sales automation to make sense?
$3K+ ACV. Below that, volume play with full AI automation. Above $50K: human-led with AI assist. The sweet spot $10-100K gets maximum leverage.
How do I handle GDPR/privacy with AI outreach?
Legitimate interest basis for B2B. Suppress EU/UK if no lawful basis. Honor opt-outs instantly. Don't use personal email for cold outreach. Document lawful basis per contact. Use business emails only.
How do I measure AI sales automation ROI?
Track: Meetings booked per $1K spend (AI vs human), AE hours saved/week, proposal turnaround time, forecast accuracy, win rate by source. Target: 3x meetings per dollar, 10+ hrs/AE/week saved.

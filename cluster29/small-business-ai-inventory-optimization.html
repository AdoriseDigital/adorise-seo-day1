Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. Over-Automating Too Early

Deploying complex AI before nailing basics (clear processes, solid data, working team) amplifies chaos. Fix: Automate one workflow at a time. Master it. Then expand.

2. Ignoring Data Quality

AI is only as good as its training data. Feeding garbage data into predictive models produces garbage predictions. Fix: Clean data first. Dedupe, standardize, enrich. Then automate.

3. Set-and-Forget Mentality

AI models drift. Business conditions change. Customer needs evolve. Fix: Monthly model review, quarterly strategy updates, continuous A/B testing.

4. Neglecting Human Oversight

AI executes, humans govern. If oversight is missing (no alerts, no audit trail, no escalation), automation risk compounds. Fix: Design governance as carefully as automation. Real-time dashboards, audit logs, human checkpoints.

5. Single-Use-Case Thinking

Automating one process in isolation misses compound value. Fix: Platform thinking from day one. Even if you start with one workflow, architect for enterprise-wide deployment.

Roadmap Overview & Philosophy

This roadmap is based on deploying AI automation for 200+ small businesses. The core philosophy: value first, perfection later. Every phase delivers measurable ROI. You never wait 90 days for value—you see it in week 1.

Results from this roadmap: Average client deploys 12-18 workflows in 90 days, achieving 25-50x ROI on tool spend. Time-to-first-value: 3 days. Full team adoption: 4-6 weeks.

Core Principles

  1. One workflow per week minimum — momentum compounds
  2. Measure from day 1 — if you can't measure it, don't build it
  3. Human-in-the-loop for high stakes — AI proposes, human approves
  4. Document everything — SOPs enable scaling and onboarding
  5. Celebrate wins publicly — culture change requires visibility

Phase 1: Foundation & Quick Wins (Days 1-30)

Goal: Deploy 3-5 high-impact workflows, prove value, build confidence.

Week 1 (Days 1-7): Lead Generation Foundation

Owner: Marketing + SalesWorkflow: Lead Capture → Enrich → Score → CRM → Alert

Daily Plan:

  • Day 1: Onboarding call with AI Automation Suite specialist. Select "Lead Capture & Qualification" template.
  • Day 2: Connect website forms/landing pages. Map fields to HubSpot.
  • Day 3: Configure enrichment (Clearbit/Apollo). Define scoring rules.
  • Day 4: Build Slack alert to #sales-leads with enrichment data.
  • Day 5: Test with 10 real submissions. Fix field mapping issues.
  • Day 6: Go live. Monitor first 24 hours closely.
  • Day 7: Review metrics. Document SOP. Celebrate first automated leads!

🎯 Week 1 Milestone

Lead response time < 5 minutes. 100% leads enriched. Sales team gets Slack alerts with full context.

Week 2 (Days 8-14): Sales Acceleration

Owner: Sales LeadWorkflows: Meeting Prep + Follow-Up Sequence

Workflow 2: Meeting Prep Automation

  • Trigger: Calendar event created (Calendly/HubSpot/Google)
  • AI: Research attendee + company (LinkedIn, news, tech stack)
  • Output: Prep doc in Notion/Google Doc, delivered to rep 1 hr before

Workflow 3: Post-Meeting Follow-Up Sequence

  • Trigger: Meeting ended (calendar) or call logged (Gong/Fireflies)
  • AI: Summarize call, extract action items, draft follow-up email
  • Actions: Update CRM, create tasks, enroll in nurture sequence based on outcome

🎯 Week 2 Milestone

Reps receive prep docs for 100% of meetings. Follow-up emails sent within 30 min of every meeting.

Week 3 (Days 15-21): Operations Quick Win

Owner: Operations/FinanceWorkflow: Invoice Processing → Approval → Accounting

Build Steps:

  1. Email trigger: invoices@yourdomain.com (or dedicated inbox)
  2. AI extraction: Vendor, amount, date, line items, PO#, GL code suggestion
  3. Validation: Duplicate check, PO match, vendor status, amount limits
  4. Routing: <$500 auto-approve, $500-5k manager, >$5k director
  5. Approval: Slack message with one-click approve/reject, 24hr SLA
  6. Action: Create bill in QuickBooks/Xero, schedule payment, notify vendor
  7. Exception: Reject → notify vendor with reason, escalate to AP lead

🎯 Week 3 Milestone

Invoice processing time: 25 min → 3 min. Zero missed invoices. 100% audit trail.

Week 4 (Days 22-30): Content & Marketing Automation

Owner: MarketingWorkflows: Content Download Nurture + Social Repurposing

Workflow 4: Content Download → Nurture Sequence

  • Trigger: Lead magnet download (form submit)
  • Tag: Interest topic in CRM
  • Sequence: 5-email nurture (value → case study → demo → trial → consult)
  • Branch: High score (>70) → sales alert; Low score → continue nurture

Workflow 5: Blog Post → Social Content Repurposing

  • Trigger: New blog post published (RSS/webhook)
  • AI: Generate 5 LinkedIn posts, 10 tweets, 1 newsletter section
  • Review: Human approval in Slack (one-click)
  • Schedule: Auto-post to Buffer/Hootsuite/LinkedIn

🎯 Week 4 Milestone

Every content download triggers nurture. Every blog post generates 16 social assets automatically.

Phase 1 Exit Criteria (Day 30)

  • 5 workflows live in production
  • Lead response time < 5 minutes (measured)
  • Invoice processing time < 5 minutes (measured)
  • Sales team using prep docs for 100% of meetings
  • Content nurture converting downloads → MQLs at >15%
  • Team adoption > 80% (measured by workflow usage)
  • Phase 2 plan approved with specific workflows

Phase 2: Core Automation Deployment (Days 31-60)

Goal: Deploy 5-8 workflows covering sales, marketing, customer success. Connect workflows cross-functionally.

Week 5-6 (Days 31-42): Sales Process Automation

Workflows: Outbound Sequences, Proposal Gen, Pipeline Updates

Workflow 6: Signal-Based Outbound Sequences

  • Trigger: New signal detected (Clay/Apollo/6sense → webhook)
  • Enrich: Full company + contact profile
  • AI: Generate personalized sequence (email + LinkedIn + call script)
  • Send: Via Instantly/Smartlead or Suite email
  • Track: Opens, clicks, replies → update CRM score

Workflow 7: Proposal Generation

  • Trigger: Deal stage = "Proposal" in CRM
  • AI: Populate template with company data, pricing, terms, case studies
  • Review: Rep reviews in PandaDoc/Qwilr, clicks send
  • Track: Opens, time spent, forwards → alert rep

Workflow 8: Pipeline Hygiene Automation

  • Daily: Scan deals >14 days no activity → alert owner
  • Weekly: Forecast confidence scoring based on activity
  • Monthly: Stale deal cleanup suggestions

Week 7-8 (Days 43-54): Customer Success Automation

Workflows: Onboarding, Health Scoring, Renewal Prep

Workflow 9: Customer Onboarding Sequence

  • Trigger: Deal closed-won
  • Create: Onboarding project in Asana/Monday, provision accounts
  • Sequence: Welcome → Setup → Training → First Value → Expansion
  • Monitor: Adoption metrics, stall detection → alert CSM

Workflow 10: Health Scoring & Churn Prevention

  • Daily: Calculate health score (usage, support, NPS, billing, engagement)
  • Alert: Score drops >10 points → CSM Slack alert with playbook
  • Playbook: Automated sequence based on risk factor

Workflow 11: Renewal Preparation

  • Trigger: 90/60/30 days before renewal
  • AI: Generate renewal deck with usage, ROI, expansion opportunities
  • Actions: Schedule QBR, send deck, create expansion tasks

Week 9-10 (Days 55-60): Marketing Scale & Integration

Workflows: ABM, Event Follow-Up, Competitive Intel

Workflow 12: ABM Account Orchestration

  • Tier 1 (20 accounts): Custom landing pages, direct mail triggers, exec outreach
  • Tier 2 (50 accounts): Personalized video, targeted ads, multi-threaded sequences
  • Tier 3 (100 accounts): Industry content, automated nurture, retargeting

Workflow 13: Event Lead Follow-Up

  • Trigger: Event attendee list upload / badge scan
  • Enrich: Full profiles, company info, intent signals
  • Route: Assign to territory rep, start personalized sequence
  • Track: Engagement → MQL conversion → pipeline attribution

Workflow 14: Competitive Intelligence

  • Daily: Monitor competitor pricing, features, content, reviews
  • AI: Summarize changes, assess threat level, draft battlecard updates
  • Deliver: Weekly digest to sales + product, real-time Slack alerts for major moves

Phase 2 Exit Criteria (Day 60)

  • 10-13 workflows live in production
  • Sales using outbound sequences for 100% of new prospects
  • Proposal generation time < 15 minutes
  • Customer health scores updating daily, alerts firing
  • Onboarding sequence active for 100% of new customers
  • ABM workflows running for Tier 1+2 accounts
  • Cross-functional data flowing: Marketing MQL → Sales → CS
  • Phase 3 plan approved

Phase 3: Integration & Intelligence (Days 61-90)

Goal: Connect everything, add predictive intelligence, establish governance, hit 15-18 workflows.

Week 11-12 (Days 61-72): Cross-Functional Chaining

Focus: End-to-end workflows spanning Marketing→Sales→CS→Finance

Workflow 15: Lead-to-Cash Automation

  • Marketing: Lead captured → enriched → scored → nurtured
  • Sales: MQL → meeting → proposal → negotiation → closed-won
  • CS: Onboarding → adoption → health monitoring → expansion
  • Finance: Contract → billing → revenue recognition → renewal
  • Automation: Each stage triggers next, data flows bidirectionally

Workflow 16: Referral Program Automation

  • Trigger: NPS 9+ or renewal or expansion
  • Ask: Automated referral request with unique link
  • Track: Referral visits → signups → conversions → rewards
  • Reward: Auto-fulfill credits/swag/cash via Tremendous/API

Week 13-14 (Days 73-84): Predictive Intelligence Layer

Focus: AI that predicts, not just executes

Workflow 17: Predictive Lead Scoring v2

  • Train: Use 6 months of closed-won/lost data
  • Features: Firmographic + behavioral + intent + engagement
  • Deploy: Model scores every lead in real-time
  • Feedback: Monthly retrain, feature importance review

Workflow 18: Revenue Forecasting

  • Input: Pipeline, historical conversion, seasonality, rep capacity
  • Model: Probabilistic forecast with confidence intervals
  • Output: Weekly forecast vs quota, risk deals, coaching priorities
  • Alert: Forecast deviation >10% → leadership Slack

Workflow 19: Capacity Planning

  • Input: Pipeline, close rates, implementation capacity, hiring plan
  • Output: 90-day capacity forecast, hiring triggers, contractor needs

Week 15-16 (Days 85-90): Governance & Optimization

Focus: Sustainable operations, continuous improvement

Governance Setup:

  • Automation CoE charter (even if 1 person)
  • Change management process: request → review → test → deploy → monitor
  • Naming conventions, folder structure, version control (Git)
  • Monitoring dashboard: success rates, error rates, queue depths, costs
  • Monthly health review cadence

Optimization Sprint:

  • Audit all 15-18 workflows: kill zombies, optimize expensive, fix flaky
  • A/B test results review: implement winners
  • Cost optimization: consolidate tools, right-size plans
  • Q4 roadmap: prioritize next 10 workflows

🎯 Phase 3 Milestone (Day 90)

15-18 workflows live. End-to-end lead-to-cash automated. Predictive models running. Governance established. Team self-sufficient.

Phase 3 Exit Criteria (Day 90)

  • 15-18 workflows live, all monitored
  • Lead-to-cash flow automated end-to-end
  • Predictive scoring model in production (>70% accuracy)
  • Revenue forecasting automated (weekly)
  • Automation CoE operational with monthly reviews
  • Change management process documented and used
  • Quarterly roadmap published
  • ROI report: actual vs projected, presented to leadership
  • Team can build/deploy workflows independently

Common Risks & Mitigation

⚠️ Risk: Scope Creep in Pilots

Symptom: Pilot expands from 3 steps to 20, never launches.

Mitigation: Strict 2-week timebox. MVP scope frozen at kickoff. Park enhancements for v2.

⚠️ Risk: Data Quality Blocks Automation

Symptom: Enrichment fails, scoring wrong, CRM duplicates.

Mitigation: Clean data BEFORE automation. Dedup, standardize, enrich. Run data quality report weekly.

⚠️ Risk: Team Resistance / Low Adoption

Symptom: Team reverts to manual, ignores alerts, doesn't use new tools.

Mitigation: Co-design with end users. 30-min training per workflow. Celebrate wins. Measure adoption weekly.

⚠️ Risk: Single Point of Failure

Symptom: One person knows everything. They get sick/leave → chaos.

Mitigation: Pair program everything. Document in Notion. Cross-train 2nd person per workflow.

⚠️ Risk: Tool Sprawl

Symptom: 15 tools, $3k/mo, none integrated, data silos.

Mitigation: Default to AI Automation Suite. Add point tools ONLY for proven gaps. Quarterly tool audit.

⚠️ Risk: Security/Compliance Gaps

Symptom: PII in logs, unauthorized access, vendor risk.

Mitigation: DLP rules, secret rotation, vendor security reviews, quarterly access audits.

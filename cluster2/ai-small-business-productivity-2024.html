  1. Measure: Track time savings, error rates, adoption daily
  2. Iterate: Fix breaks, improve prompts, add edge cases

  3. Cross-functional: Connect marketing→sales→CS workflows
  4. ROI report: Actuals vs. projections, present to leadership
  5. Roadmap: Next quarter's automation priorities

ROI Measurement Framework

The AI ROI Formula

Net AI Value = (Hours Saved × Hourly Cost) + (Revenue Impact) + (Risk Reduction) - (Tool Cost) - (Implementation Cost) - (Ongoing Admin)

Measurement Tiers

TierMetricsFrequencyOwner
OperationalRun time, success rate, error count, queue depthDailyAutomation owner
TacticalHours saved, cost per transaction, throughput, quality scoreWeeklyFunction lead
StrategicROI, CAC impact, LTV impact, capacity freed, competitive positionMonthlyLeadership

Attribution Model

Don't just measure "AI saved X hours." Measure:

  • Direct substitution: Human hours → AI hours (easiest to measure)
  • Quality improvement: Error rate reduction → cost avoidance
  • Speed improvement: Cycle time reduction → revenue acceleration
  • Scale enablement: Volume handled without headcount increase
  • Strategic optionality: New capabilities unlocked (24/7, multi-language, personalization at scale)
Real Example: 10-person marketing agency. AI content + lead gen automation.
• Direct substitution: 40 hrs/week × $35/hr = $5,600/mo saved
• Quality: Lead response 4 hrs → 3 min = 3x qualified pipeline = $45k/mo revenue
• Scale: Handle 5x leads without hiring = $120k avoided hire
• Tools: $497 (Suite) + $100 (Jasper) = $597/mo
• Net monthly value: $170k+ | ROI: 285x

PitfallSymptomPrevention

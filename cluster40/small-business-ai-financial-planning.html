Phase 1: Discovery & Mapping (Week 1)

  1. Audit current processes — document every step, decision point, and handoff
  2. Identify highest-impact, highest-volume workflows
  3. Map data sources, systems, and stakeholders
  4. Define success metrics and guardrails

Phase 2: Pilot Deployment (Weeks 2-3)

  1. Configure AI agents for your specific use case
  2. Connect to existing tools via API/integration layer

Phase 3: Full Rollout (Week 4+)

  1. Gradually shift volume to AI with human oversight
  2. Implement feedback loops for continuous improvement
  3. Expand to adjacent workflows

Small Business Tools Stack 2024: Complete Software Suite

The curated stack for CRM, marketing, finance, operations, and AI. Every tool chosen for integration, ROI, and ability to scale from 5 to 500 people.

2024 Updated 15 min read Adorise Digital

The average small business uses 17 SaaS tools but only integrates 3. This stack gives you 9 integrated layers that talk to each other—data flows, not data silos.

Stack Philosophy

  • API-first: Every tool must have a real API (not just Zapier)
  • Usage-based or fair pricing: No per-seat traps that punish growth
  • Native integrations > Zapier: Direct connections are faster, cheaper, more reliable
  • AI-native preferred: Tools building AI in, not bolting on

Layer 1: CRM (Revenue Engine)

Primary: Attio
$18-45/user/mo

Modern, flexible, API-first. Custom objects, lists, and views replace rigid pipelines. Best for B2B services, agencies, SaaS.

Alternative: HubSpot Free - $1,500+/mo

If you need marketing automation built-in and have budget. Steep price jumps at scale.

Alternative: Pipedrive $14-99/user/mo

Sales-focused, visual pipeline. Good for productized services with standard sales cycles.

Layer 2: Marketing & Email

Primary: Kit (formerly ConvertKit)
$0-59/mo

Creator-focused but powerful for any business. Visual automations, tag-based segmentation, landing pages, paid newsletter. No per-contact pricing.

Alternative: Customer.io $150+/mo

Event-based, developer-friendly. Best for product-led SaaS with complex lifecycle flows.

Alternative: ActiveCampaign $29-149+/mo

Advanced automation, CRM lite, site tracking. Good middle ground.

Layer 3: Finance & Billing

Accounting: Xero
$15-78/mo

Best bank feeds, multi-currency, accountant ecosystem. API for custom integrations.

Alternative: QuickBooks Online $30-200/mo

US market leader. More integrations, but API access limited on lower tiers.

Billing/Subscriptions: Stripe
2.9% + 30¢

Gold standard. Billing portal, subscriptions, invoicing, tax calculation. Works globally.

Alternative: Paddle 5% + 50¢

Merchant of record—handles global tax/VAT. Best for digital products selling internationally.

Layer 4: Operations & Project Management

Primary: Linear
Free - $12/user/mo

Fast, opinionated, keyboard-first. Issues, projects, cycles, roadmaps. Best for product/engineering teams.

Alternative: Notion Free - $10/user/mo

All-in-one: docs, wiki, projects, CRM lite. Best for knowledge-heavy teams (agencies, consultancies).

Alternative: ClickUp Free - $12/user/mo

Everything platform. Customizable but can be overwhelming. Good free tier.

Layer 5: Communication

Internal: Slack
Free - $12.50/user/mo

Still the best for team chat. Workflow builder, huddles, canvas, 2600+ apps. Free tier generous.

External/Video: Loom
Free - $15/user/mo

Async video replaces 50% of meetings. AI titles, chapters, tasks. Essential for remote/hybrid.

Layer 6: Automation & Integration (The Glue)

Primary: n8n (self-hosted)
Free (server cost only)

400+ nodes, code nodes, webhooks, self-hosted = data control, no task limits. Best for complex logic.

Cloud: n8n Cloud $50/mo

Managed hosting. Worth it if no DevOps capacity.

Alternative: Make Free - $10.59/mo

Visual, great debugger, generous free tier. Operations-based pricing scales fair.

Layer 8: File Storage & Collaboration

Primary: Google Workspace
$6-18/user/mo

Email, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Meet, Calendar. Universal compatibility.

Free

SQL-based dashboards, embeddable, alerts, subscriptions. Connects to any DB.

Product analytics, session replay, feature flags, A/B testing. Self-hostable.

FAQ

Do I need all 9 layers from day one?
No. Start with CRM + Email + Accounting + Automation. Add layers as pain points emerge. The stack is designed to integrate incrementally.
Why n8n over Zapier for automation?
Zapier charges per task. At scale (10k+ tasks/mo), Zapier costs $500+. n8n self-hosted is free unlimited. n8n also handles complex logic (loops, branching, code) that Zapier can't.
What if I'm not technical—can I use n8n?
n8n has a learning curve. Start with Make (visual, easier) or Zapier (easiest). Migrate to n8n when you hit complexity or cost limits. AI Automation Suite includes n8n templates you can import.

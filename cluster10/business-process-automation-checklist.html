FAQ

Should I score every process or just the top candidates?
Score your top 10 candidates. The checklist takes 10-15 min per process. Don't score obvious non-candidates (annual processes, highly variable creative work).
What if a process scores high on pain but low on feasibility?
That's a process redesign opportunity, not automation. Fix the data/access/logic gaps first, then re-score.
Can I use this checklist for AI agent workflows?
Yes. For AI agents, add: "Training data available," "Evaluation criteria defined," "Human escalation path clear," "Cost per execution acceptable."

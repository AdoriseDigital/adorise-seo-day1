ROI Formula Breakdown

Our calculator uses this conservative formula:

Monthly Labor Savings

Employees × Hourly Cost × Hours/Week × 4.33 × 60% = Monthly Labor Savings

60% time reduction is conservative — actual range 50-80% depending on workflow type.

Monthly Error Reduction Savings

Employees × Hours/Week × 4.33 × (Error Rate ÷ 100) × Cost Per Error × 80% = Error Savings

AI reduces structured task errors by ~80%. Typical manual error rate: 10-20%.

Opportunity Recapture

Estimated Missed Revenue × 50% = Opportunity Recapture

Conservative: automation frees capacity for 50% of previously missed opportunities.

Net ROI

Total Monthly Savings - $497 (AI Automation Suite) = Net Monthly ROI
ROI Multiple = Total Monthly Savings ÷ $497

Foundation: ICP, Offer, Funnel

Before tactics, nail these three. Everything else amplifies them—or wastes money.

1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) — Be Ruthlessly Specific

"Small businesses" is not an ICP. "B2B SaaS companies, 10-50 employees, $1-10M ARR, using HubSpot, hiring for sales roles, frustrated with manual lead qualification" — that's an ICP.

ICP Template:

  • Firmographics: Industry, size, revenue, geography, tech stack
  • Psychographics: Values, buying triggers, decision process, risk tolerance
  • Situation: Current problems, attempted solutions, budget cycle, urgency
  • Negative signals: Who you explicitly DON'T serve (saves massive waste)

2. Irresistible Offer — Solve a Bleeding Neck Problem

Your offer isn't your product. It's the packaged outcome with risk reversal.

Weak Offer Strong Offer
"AI automation software" "3x qualified leads in 90 days or don't pay"
"Marketing consulting" "$50K pipeline in 60 days, we do the work"
"CRM implementation" "Sales team productive in 2 weeks, guaranteed"

Outbound Strategies (Push)

Outbound controls volume and timing. Higher CAC, faster feedback, precise targeting. Essential for early stage or new markets.

OUTBOUND

1. Signal-Based Outbound

Don't cold email lists. Email triggers: funding announcements, hiring for relevant roles, tech stack changes, competitor reviews, website visits, content engagement.

Tools: Apollo, Clay, RB2B, Factors.ai + AI Automation Suite for orchestration.

OUTBOUND

2. Account-Based Marketing (ABM) Lite

Pick 50-100 dream accounts. Research deeply. Multi-thread: LinkedIn connect → personalized video → relevant case study → executive outreach → direct mail. Orchestrate across channels.

OUTBOUND

3. AI-Personalized Cold Email

Not "Hi {first_name}". AI researches each prospect: recent posts, company news, tech stack, hiring, competitors. Writes unique email referencing specific context. 3-5x reply rates.

Framework: Trigger → Research → Personalize → Value prop → Soft CTA → Follow-up sequence.

OUTBOUND

4. LinkedIn Social Selling System

Daily: 20 connection requests (personalized), 30 comments, 5 DM conversations. Weekly: 3 value posts. Monthly: 1 live/event. Track: SSI score, conversations started, meetings booked.

OUTBOUND

5. Retargeting & Paid Amplification

Retarget website visitors (30/60/90 day). Lookalike audiences from customers. Lead gen forms on LinkedIn/Meta. Budget: 20% of total lead gen spend.

Referral & Partner Strategies

Highest conversion, lowest CAC, most neglected. Systematize it.

REFERRAL

1. Customer Referral Program

Ask at peak happiness (post-onboarding, renewal, success milestone). Make it easy: one-click share, pre-written messages, trackable links. Reward: account credit, swag, charity donation.

REFERRAL

2. Partner/Channel Program

Identify 10-20 agencies, consultants, freelancers serving your ICP. Formalize: referral fee (10-20% first year), co-marketing, joint webinars, dedicated support.

REFERRAL

3. Investor/Advisor Network

Your investors/advisors know 100s of founders. Quarterly: send 3-sentence update + 1 specific ask ("Intro to Series A SaaS founders hiring VP Sales").

REFERRAL

4. User-Generated Content & Case Studies

Turn customers into advocates. Video testimonials, written case studies, podcast appearances, referral links in their email signatures.

Building Your Channel Mix

Don't do everything. Pick 3-4 channels based on your stage:

Stage Primary (60%) Secondary (30%) Tertiary (10%)
Pre-PMF (<$10K MRR) Founder-led outbound + network Content (LinkedIn) Referrals
Early Growth ($10-100K MRR) Signal-based outbound + SEO ABM + partnerships Referral program
Scale ($100K+ MRR) SEO + paid + AI automation ABM + partnerships Referrals + brand
Critical: Every channel needs an owner, a metric, a budget, and a review cadence. If you can't measure it, don't do it.

Measurement & Optimization

North Star Metrics

  • Cost Per Qualified Lead (CPQL): All-in cost / SQLs
  • Lead Velocity Rate: Month-over-month growth in qualified leads
  • Channel Payback Period: Months to recover CAC per channel
  • Pipeline per Channel: $ pipeline generated per channel

Leading Indicators (Weekly)

  • New leads by channel
  • Lead-to-MQL rate by channel
  • Response time (target: <5 min)
  • Content engagement (inbound)
  • Reply rate (outbound)

Lagging Indicators (Monthly)

  • MQL-to-SQL conversion
  • Sales cycle length
  • Average deal size
  • CAC by channel
  • LTV:CAC ratio (target: >3:1)

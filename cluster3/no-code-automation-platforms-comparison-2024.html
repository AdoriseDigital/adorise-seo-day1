Evaluation Criteria: What Actually Matters

Don't evaluate on feature checklists. Evaluate on your constraints:

🎯 Time to First Value

How fast from signup to working workflow in production? Templates matter more than flexibility here.

🔧 Maintenance Burden

How often do workflows break? How hard to debug? Self-healing? Version control?

💰 Cost at Scale

Per-execution pricing kills budgets. Look for workspace-based or included executions.

🤖 AI That Works

Not "has AI" but "AI that reliably does X." Test: classification, extraction, generation, reasoning.

🔐 Data Control

Self-host option? Data residency? SOC2? Your data leaving your VPC?

👥 Team Adoption

Can non-technical people build? Or only "the automation person"?

Pricing Deep Dive & Hidden Costs

The Pricing Models

  • Per-task/operation: Zapier, Make — predictable at low volume, explodes at scale
  • Per-seat: Enterprise iPaaS — scales with team, not usage
  • Per-agent/workflow: AI-native — scales with automation scope
  • Workspace/flat: AI Automation Suite, n8n self-hosted — predictable, unlimited

Decision Matrix: Which to Choose

Choose AI Automation Suite if:

  • You want outcomes in days, not months
  • You need lead gen + sales + marketing automation
  • You want pre-built, tested workflows (20+ industries)
  • You want predictable flat pricing with AI included
  • You want white-glove onboarding and support

Choose Zapier if:

  • You need a rare integration only Zapier has
  • Your workflows are simple (2-3 steps, linear)
  • Your team is non-technical and already knows Zapier
  • Volume is low (<5K tasks/mo)

Choose Make if:

  • You need complex data transformation (arrays, iterators)
  • You prefer visual debugging
  • You need more operations than Zapier allows at price point
  • You have semi-technical users who can learn

Choose n8n if:

  • You have developers who can maintain it
  • You need self-hosting (data privacy, VPC)
  • You want to build custom AI agents with LangChain
  • You want to avoid per-execution pricing at scale

Choose Lindy/Relevance if:

  • You need autonomous agents, not workflows
  • You have budget for experimentation ($500-1000+/mo)
  • You have technical capacity for agent design
  • You need multi-agent orchestration

Migration & Multi-Platform Strategy

You Don't Have to Pick Just One

Most mature teams use 2-3 platforms for different purposes:

  • AI Automation Suite for core business workflows (lead gen, sales, marketing, ops)
  • n8n (self-hosted) for custom AI agents, data-intensive workflows, internal tools
  • Zapier/Make for rare one-off integrations, personal productivity

Migration Path (If Switching)

  1. Inventory: Export all workflows, document triggers/actions/data mappings
  2. Prioritize: Move highest-impact, simplest workflows first
  3. Parallel run: New platform in shadow mode 2 weeks
  4. Cutover: Switch traffic 10% → 50% → 100% over 1 week
  5. Decommission: Cancel old platform after 30 days stable
Pro tip: Build workflows in a platform-agnostic way: document the logic, data flows, and business rules separately from implementation. Makes migration trivial.
